PinDark — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-07-24

PinDark is a browser extension that applies a dark theme to Pinterest Web. This page describes what data PinDark collects, stores, and transmits.

Data collection & Transparency

PinDark does not collect any data. The extension does not run analytics, telemetry, tracking pixels, or any form of user monitoring. It does not make network requests of its own. The only network traffic generated by PinDark originates from the Pinterest page itself, which is loaded independently of the extension.

To ensure complete transparency and verify these privacy claims, PinDark is fully open-source and released under the GNU GPLv3 license. Anyone can review the source code on GitHub.

Data storage

PinDark stores the following preferences locally using chrome.storage.sync so that your settings follow you across devices signed in to the same browser:

  • Whether dark mode is enabled (boolean)
  • Whether the schedule is enabled (boolean)
  • Schedule start time (hour and minute)
  • Schedule end time (hour and minute)

These values never leave your browser. They are not transmitted to any server.

Permissions

PinDark requests the following permissions, used only for the stated purposes:

  • storage — to save your preferences locally and sync them across your devices.
  • alarms — to re-evaluate the schedule on a 15-minute tick.
  • Host permission for *://*.pinterest.com/* — to inject the dark theme into Pinterest pages only.

Third parties

PinDark does not share data with any third party. The only external link in the extension is a "Support" button that opens the developer's Topmate page in a new tab when you click it; clicking the button is entirely your choice.

Children's privacy

PinDark does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If PinDark ever changes its data practices, this page will be updated and the change will be reflected in the extension's changelog.
