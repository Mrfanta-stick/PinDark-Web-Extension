## Short description (132 chars max)

A clean, lightweight dark mode for Pinterest Web. Toggle on, schedule it for the night, and never get blinded by the feed.

## Long description

PinDark gives Pinterest Web a proper dark theme that respects the design of every page — the home feed, search, pin detail modals, your profile, boards, and settings.

**What you get**

- Dark mode across the entire Pinterest experience, including the home feed, search, pin detail, profile pages, boards, and settings.
- A single on/off toggle in the toolbar popup.
- An optional schedule that turns dark mode on automatically during the hours you choose (perfect for evening browsing).
- A live status indicator so you can see at a glance whether dark mode is currently Active, Scheduled, or Off.
- A Support link if you find the extension useful.

**How it works**

PinDark overrides Pinterest's design tokens (the CSS variables behind the colours on every page) so the dark theme applies consistently without breaking images, videos, or animations. Unlike many dark-mode extensions, PinDark does not invert the page, which means photos stay accurate and nothing flickers when you scroll.

**Privacy**

PinDark does not collect any data. There is no analytics, no tracking, and no network requests. Your preferences (toggle and schedule) are stored locally in your browser and synced across your devices via the browser's own storage sync.
